## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor): This dimension asks whether the study, as designed and analysed, can actually answer the questions it poses at the standard a careful empirical reviewer in machine-learning evaluation would apply. From my seat that means four things checked together: the design (what was varied, what was held fixed, what the unit of analysis and the unit of replication are), the data handling (how evaluation units, runs and exclusions were defined and whether any were chosen after seeing outcomes), the statistical reporting (test choice, effect sizes with intervals, treatment of run-to-run noise, multiplicity, and the difference between rules fixed in advance and analyses added later), and the affordances for reproduction (code, configurations, seeds, data and logs sufficient for an independent rerun). It is mandatory and I am its owner, so my score here can by itself drive the panel outcome.

D2 (domain_accuracy): This dimension asks whether the factual and technical content is right for the field: that statements match current evidence, that earlier work is described as it actually is, and that field-specific terms, benchmarks and reported results are used correctly. It is mandatory but belongs to the domain seat. I do not score it; where a methodological observation of mine depends on a domain fact I cannot judge, I will say so and leave the judgement to that seat.

D3 (argumentative_coherence): This dimension asks whether the central argument holds together: the thesis does not contradict itself across sections, each claim is carried by evidence of matching strength and scope, and no reasoning error sits under the main conclusion. From a methodology viewpoint the relevant question is the fit between what the design licenses and what the text concludes, including whether causal or general wording outruns a design that supports only a narrower or associational statement, and whether confirmatory and exploratory results are kept apart when conclusions are drawn. It is mandatory, owned by the devil's advocate seat, and I am eligible to score it alongside that seat.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance): This dimension asks whether a reader from a neighbouring field can follow the framing and definitions and whether any claims that reach across disciplines are backed up. It is a high-priority dimension owned by the perspective seat. I do not score it; my only contact with it is that statistical terms should be defined clearly enough for non-specialists, which I would raise as a rigor or clarity point under my own dimensions.

D5 (writing_and_structure): This dimension asks about organisation, clarity of exposition, quality of figures and tables, and conformity to the conventions of the outlet. It has normal priority and is owned by the editor-in-chief seat. I do not score it; presentation matters to me only where it prevents a reader from reconstructing the analysis, and that I would record under methodology rigor.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution): This dimension asks whether the work suits the configured outlet and offers an original contribution of adequate weight for its readers. It is mandatory and owned by the editor-in-chief seat. I do not score it, and since this call carries no criteria binding I make no statement about alignment with any outlet.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Whether each analysis rule presented as fixed in advance has a dated, verifiable record that precedes the data it governs, and whether any interim looks are disclosed with their consequences for inference; whether the wording used for pre-specification matches what was actually done; whether the unit of analysis and unit of replication are correctly identified and the tests respect pairing and dependence; whether run-to-run noise is quantified and carried into the uncertainty of headline estimates; whether effect sizes and intervals accompany every test; whether confirmatory and post hoc analyses are separated and multiplicity is handled or openly declared unadjusted; whether the number of training seeds and the number of factors varied together are stated and reflected in the scope of the inference; whether exclusions, stopping rules, code, configurations and data are documented well enough for an independent rerun.
what_triggers_block: A headline quantitative claim rests on an analysis whose rule cannot be shown to predate its data while being presented as confirmatory; or the statistical test violates the dependence structure of the data in a way that could change the conclusion; or a headline result holds in only part of the replicated evaluation and is reported as established without that qualification; or post hoc unadjusted tests are used as primary support for a core claim; or the information needed to recompute the headline results is missing; in each case the defect is repairable by re-analysis, re-labelling, added reporting or narrowed claims.
what_triggers_warn: Reporting gaps that do not threaten the headline conclusion, namely missing effect sizes or intervals on secondary results, incomplete description of exclusions or interim looks whose impact is plausibly small, unadjusted exploratory tests that are labelled as exploratory but not counted, pre-specification wording stronger than the record supports while the analysis itself is sound, or reproducibility materials that exist but are incomplete.
what_triggers_fatal: The design cannot support the core claim under any re-analysis of the existing data, for example because outcome-dependent selection of data, rules or runs is demonstrated and not separable from the reported effect, or because the reported effect is indistinguishable from run-to-run or seed-to-seed variation and no retained data can resolve it, or because reported results cannot be reconciled with the described procedure, so that only a new study could establish the claim.

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the conclusions drawn match what the design licenses, in particular whether a claim attributed to one manipulated factor is separable from other factors that changed with it, whether generality is claimed beyond the seeds, models, conditions and evaluation units actually sampled, whether both sides of any claimed trade-off are each supported by evidence of adequate strength rather than one side carrying the other, whether exploratory findings are kept out of confirmatory conclusions, whether results that fail a pre-set criterion are reported as such in the abstract and conclusion, and whether limitations stated in one section are honoured in the claims of another.
what_triggers_block: The central thesis is stated in causal or general terms that the design does not license, for instance attributing an effect to a single factor when several were varied together, or generalising from a single training run, and the title, abstract or conclusion rely on that stronger reading; or one side of a claimed two-sided relation lacks support that survives the stated noise level yet is asserted at equal strength; the argument can be made coherent by narrowing or re-wording the claims to what the evidence carries.
what_triggers_warn: Local overstatement that leaves the central thesis intact, such as isolated sentences that read exploratory results as confirmed, limitations acknowledged but not propagated to every summary statement, hedged and unhedged versions of the same claim in different sections, or an inferential step that is plausible but not argued.
what_triggers_fatal: The central thesis contradicts the manuscript's own reported evidence or depends on a fallacy that no re-wording can remove, such that once the claim is reduced to what the evidence supports no thesis of the stated kind remains.
