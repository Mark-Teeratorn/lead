## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor): This dimension asks whether the design, the handling of data, the statistical reporting and the means offered for reproduction would survive the scrutiny normal for the field. It is mandatory, and it is owned and scored by the methodology seat, not by me. From the adversarial side I treat it as a source of attack surface only: wherever a design choice, an analysis decision or an unreported degree of freedom could by itself produce the headline result, I raise that as a challenge and leave the score to the eligible seat.

D2 (domain_accuracy): This dimension asks whether the claims agree with what the field currently knows, whether earlier work is described fairly, and whether domain terms and reported results are used without factual error. It is mandatory and belongs to the domain seat; I do not score it. My adversarial reading of it is narrower: I look for prior work that is characterised in a way that makes the contribution look larger than it is, and for counter-evidence in the field that a claim would have to answer, and I report such points as challenges for the domain seat to weigh.

D3 (argumentative_coherence): This dimension asks whether the central thesis holds together, whether the evidence offered actually carries the claims built on it, and whether any fallacy damages the main argument. It is mandatory, I am its owner, and I share scoring eligibility with the methodology seat. This is where my adversarial work is concentrated: I build the strongest counter-argument I can against the thesis, follow each inferential step from evidence to conclusion looking for the weakest link, test whether rival explanations are excluded or merely unmentioned, and check whether the conclusions reach further than the evidence does.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance): This dimension asks whether framing, definitions and implications can be followed by readers from neighbouring fields, and whether claims that cross disciplinary lines are backed up. It has high priority and is scored by the perspective seat alone. Adversarially, I ask which affected parties or neighbouring viewpoints are left out and whether any claim borrowed from another field is asserted without support; these go forward as challenges, not as a score.

D5 (writing_and_structure): This dimension covers organisation, clarity of exposition, the quality of figures and tables, and conformity with the conventions of the target outlet. It has normal priority and is scored by the editor-in-chief seat. I do not review prose or layout; the only point at which it touches my role is when unclear wording or an ambiguous figure hides a gap in the argument, in which case I report the gap under my own dimension and leave the presentational matter to the eligible seat.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution): This dimension asks whether the work suits the configured outlet and offers an original contribution of sufficient weight for its readers. It is mandatory and scored by the editor-in-chief seat only. I make no judgement of fit here, and no outlet criteria have been supplied to this call. My adversarial contribution is limited to the question of consequence: if the central claim were granted in full, what would change for a reader, and is that stated or only implied; any such challenge is passed to the eligible seat without a score from me.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the central thesis is stated in a form that could be shown wrong; whether every step from evidence to conclusion is explicit and holds; whether the scope of the conclusions matches the scope of the evidence; whether rival explanations are named and tested; whether the selection of evidence, measures and comparisons is justified against alternatives that could point the other way; whether terms keep one meaning from premise to conclusion; whether stated limitations are consistent with the strength of the claims made elsewhere; and whether the consequence of the result is argued or only asserted.
what_triggers_block: A claim on which the central thesis depends is not supported by the evidence presented for it, or a plausible rival explanation of the main result is left unaddressed although the reported material could separate the two, or the main conclusions extend well beyond what the evidence covers, such that the argument cannot stand as written but could be repaired by added analysis, narrowed claims or restructured reasoning.
what_triggers_warn: The central argument holds, but a secondary claim is stated more strongly than its support allows, an inferential step is left implicit, a rival explanation is acknowledged without being tested, a term shifts meaning in a way that does not change the conclusion, or the consequence of the result is asserted with little argument, such that the weakness is local and correctable by rewording or a modest addition.
what_triggers_fatal: The central thesis is internally contradictory, or it rests on a fallacy or circular definition that makes the main conclusion follow regardless of the evidence, or the evidence presented in fact supports the opposite of the main conclusion, such that no revision short of replacing the thesis itself would restore the argument.
