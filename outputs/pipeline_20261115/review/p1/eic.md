## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor): From the editor's chair this dimension asks whether the way a study was designed, the way its data were collected and processed, the way its statistics are reported, and the materials offered for reproduction would hold up under the scrutiny a careful referee in the field normally applies. It is a mandatory dimension owned by the methodology seat; I do not score it, but I rely on that seat's verdict when weighing whether the conclusions an editor is asked to publish rest on sound procedure.

D2 (domain_accuracy): This dimension asks whether what a submission asserts agrees with the present state of knowledge in its field, whether earlier work is described fairly and correctly, and whether technical terms and reported results are used without factual error. It is mandatory and belongs to the domain seat; I do not score it, and I treat that seat's finding as the authority on whether a submission can be trusted by specialist readers.

D3 (argumentative_coherence): This dimension asks whether the central thesis hangs together, whether each claim is actually carried by the evidence offered for it, and whether the reasoning is free of fallacies that would undo the main argument. It is mandatory, owned by the devil's-advocate seat and also open to the methodology seat; I do not score it, although its outcome bears directly on whether a headline message deserves an editor's endorsement.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance): This dimension asks whether the framing, the definitions and the stated implications can be followed by readers from neighbouring fields, and whether any claim that reaches beyond the home discipline is backed up rather than merely asserted. It carries high priority and is owned by the perspective seat; I do not score it, but I take it as the signal for how far beyond a specialist audience a submission can travel.

D5 (writing_and_structure): This dimension asks whether a manuscript is organised sensibly, explained clearly, supported by figures and tables that are legible and do their job, and written in line with the conventions a scholarly outlet expects. It has normal priority and I own it. Since this call is not bound to a venue, I will judge conventions in general scholarly terms for the stated field and not against any specific outlet's rules.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution): This dimension asks whether a manuscript suits the outlet it is configured for and whether it offers something original and significant enough for that readership. It is mandatory and I own it. No venue criteria are supplied in this call, so I will assess only the field-general half, that is, whether the contribution is original, significant and stated in proportion to its support, and I will make no statement about alignment with any particular venue.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Logical section order with a traceable line from question to design to results to conclusion; title, abstract and conclusion that say the same thing as the body; length proportionate to content with no redundant passages; figures and tables that are legible, self-contained through captions, correctly referenced and consistent with the text; consistent terminology and notation; complete required statements and no unresolved placeholders or draft remnants; general scholarly conventions for the field, with no venue-specific rule applied.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition defects severe enough that a referee cannot reliably establish what was done or what is claimed, for example central results absent or left as unresolved placeholders, figures or tables that contradict the text or cannot be read, or a structure that obscures the main line of argument throughout.
what_triggers_warn: Localised clarity or presentation problems that slow the reader but leave the content recoverable, for example excess length or repetition, inconsistent terms or notation, weak captions, missing cross-references, front or end matter slightly out of step with the body, or minor incomplete required statements.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: A contribution that is stated explicitly and is distinguishable from prior work; significance to readers of the stated field argued and not merely asserted; scope of the claimed contribution matched to the scope of the evidence presented; a main message that does not depend on results not yet in hand; conclusions that follow from what is reported; assessed in field-general terms only, with no venue-alignment judgement because no venue criteria are bound to this call.
what_triggers_block: The contribution as presented is not sufficient to carry publication without substantial rework, for example the stated novelty is not separated from existing work, the claimed significance materially exceeds what the evidence can bear, or the headline message rests on results that are incomplete or still pending.
what_triggers_warn: A real contribution is present but its framing needs adjustment, for example novelty or significance overstated in places, limits on generality not acknowledged clearly enough, positioning against prior work that is thin, or a title, abstract or conclusion pitched somewhat beyond the evidence.
what_triggers_fatal: No identifiable original contribution remains once the claims are read against their support, for example the work duplicates results already established without adding to them, or its central claim is unsupported in a way that revision of the text alone could not repair.
